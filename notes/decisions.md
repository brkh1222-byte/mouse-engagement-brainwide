# Design decisions (made while the researcher was away, 2026-11-24/25)

Each entry gives the choice, the alternatives and the reason. All were made from behaviour data or simulations only, before any neural analysis. The researcher can overrule any of them before the pre-registration is committed.

| # | Decision | Alternatives considered | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **GLM-HMM fitted on each mouse's full biased/ephys history** (1,328 sessions, 937,750 trials), not only the 459 recording sessions | BWM sessions only (1–8 per mouse) | Many more trials per mouse, so states are more stable. BWM trials come from the BWM aggregate table, so indices match the neural data. |
| D2 | **Block prior is a GLM-HMM input** (Bayes-observer log-odds, hazard 1/60) | Ashwood's original 4 inputs; true block side as input | Without it, "biased" states partly absorb block-following (Mohammadi et al. 2025). A Bayes observer uses only past stimuli, so it is causal and could be known to the mouse. The Ashwood design (A90) is still fitted as a check. |
| D3 | **Own implementation** (numba forward–backward, damped-Newton M-step), not dynamax/ssm | dynamax (JAX), ssm | 938k trials × 20 initialisations × 5 values of K × cross-validation needs speed on a busy CPU. The code is validated by parameter-recovery tests. Undamped Newton diverged for mice with little data; this was caught by the EM monotonicity check and fixed with step halving. |
| D4 | **Two-stage multi-start:** 20 initialisations × 100 iterations, best 3 run to convergence | 20 full runs | Same optimum in the pilots, at about a quarter of the cost. |
| D5 | **Number of states by the 1-SE rule** on per-mouse held-out bits/trial, K = 1–5 | Fixed K = 3 (Ashwood); maximum held-out likelihood | The 1-SE rule is standard and guards against over-splitting. If it lands on K = 5 (the boundary), that is reported, and K ± 1 is a pre-registered robustness check. |
| D6 | **Per-mouse versus global parameters decided by cross-validation.** Both families are scored on the same held-out sessions, and the better one labels the trials | Always per-mouse (Ashwood) | 76 of 139 mice have fewer than 5 sessions in the Ashwood design. There, per-mouse fits overfit: at K = 4 they score 0.406 held-out bits/trial against 0.433 for global parameters. Ashwood required ≥ 30 sessions per mouse; we cannot. |
| D7 | **Engaged = every state with stimulus weight ≥ 0.5 × the largest; disengaged = the rest, pooled** | Only the single top state | At K ≥ 4 there are two high-stimulus states (weights about 8.3 and 7.2) that differ only in a small bias. Calling one of them "disengaged" would be wrong. |
| D8 | **Labels from smoothed posteriors, threshold 0.8 / 0.2.** Robustness checks use filtered posteriors and **past-only (predictive) posteriors** | Past-only posteriors as primary | Smoothed posteriors are the best estimate of the state. Their weakness: they use the current trial's choice, so a region whose pre-stimulus activity predicts the upcoming choice could gain a little. The past-only labels cannot be affected by that, so they are the control. They are noisier, which is why they are not primary. |
| D9 | **No reaction-time or `bwm_include` trial filter** | IBL's 80 ms–2 s filter | Disengaged trials are often slow or no-go, so the filter would remove the very trials of interest. |
| D10 | **Session rule:** ≥ 400 trials, ≥ 30 engaged and ≥ 30 disengaged labelled usable trials, usable motion-energy video | ≥ 20 or ≥ 50 per class | With 5 outer folds, 30 gives about 6 minority-class test trials per fold. That is the least that gives a stable AUC. Counts for other thresholds are reported. |
| D11 | **Primary statistic: ROC-AUC**, not balanced accuracy | Balanced accuracy (earlier plan) | AUC is threshold-free and stable under strong class imbalance (about 75% of trials are engaged). |
| D12 | **Primary null: session permutation** (labels from 200 other sessions of other mice, aligned by trial index); **secondary: pseudo-sessions** | Pseudo-sessions as primary (earlier plan, the IBL-prior-paper style) | Real label sequences from other sessions carry the real within-session trend towards disengagement. In simulations this null was calibrated for a region with drift only. Pseudo-sessions from a stationary GLM-HMM do not carry that trend. CLAUDE.md also names the session-permutation null. |
| D13 | **Model B (covariates regressed out on training folds) is primary; Model A is exploratory** | The two as separate confirmatory hypotheses | One confirmatory test per hypothesis keeps the multiple-testing burden clear. The earlier "H2: survives controls" is now inside H1. |
| D14 | **Time-in-session covariates in Model B** (trial spline, cumulative reward) | Leave time out | Engagement falls within sessions and recordings drift. Without these, any drifting region would decode engagement. This is conservative: a real, slowly varying engagement signal is partly removed. |
| D15 | **Region level: Fisher combination + Benjamini–Hochberg q < 0.05**; Stouffer as robustness | Stouffer as primary (TASKS.md) | Fisher is what the IBL prior paper used, and it is sensitive when only a subset of sessions carry signal. |
| D16 | **H2 groups fixed now:** MRN, SCm, PAG, MOs, ACAd versus VISp, AUDp, SSp-bfd/ul/ll/m/n | Data-driven groups | Fixing the groups in advance is what makes H2 confirmatory. |
| D17 | **Steinmetz labels from a 3-outcome GLM-HMM** fitted on all 39 sessions pooled (global parameters only) | Per-mouse fits; Steinmetz's own engagement index only | 10 mice with 2–6 sessions each is too little for per-mouse fits (see D6). The engagement index is added as a secondary comparison. |
| D18 | **Steinmetz behaviour read by streaming only the trials table** from DANDI NWB files | Download the whole files (14.7 GB) | Faster, and no spike data are read before registration. |
| D19 | **The frozen label file is committed with the pre-registration** (`results/glmhmm_states.parquet`, force-added; SHA-256 in the prereg) | Hash only | Lets anyone verify that the labels did not change after registration. |
| D20 | **Real-data decoding is blocked in code** until `preregistration/prereg.md` is committed (`src/run_decoding.prereg_committed`) | Rely on discipline | A mechanical guard against breaking blindness by accident. |
